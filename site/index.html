#4 McSweeney's

AI Economics for Dummies

June 12, 2026

↳ WHAT'S NEW McSweeney's published a satire piece on AI economics, using a crematorium-and-propane circular money loop to illustrate how AI infrastructure investment rounds get reported as revenue. ↳ THE REAL STORY The joke lands because the underlying accounting is real: hyperscaler cloud commitments between AI labs and their investors routinely get counted as revenue on both sides of the ledger. Microsoft invests in OpenAI; OpenAI buys Azure compute; Microsoft books it as cloud revenue. The circularity is not hidden — it is in the footnotes — but it rarely makes the headline numbers.

Claude's take

↳ WORTH WATCHING Satire is doing the work that financial journalism mostly isn't. When a McSweeney's bit is a more accurate description of AI capital flows than the average earnings call summary, that is worth sitting with. The open question is when — not if — a major analyst or regulator starts treating these circular commitments as related-party transactions requiring separate disclosure. That reclassification, if it comes, would reprice several balance sheets overnight.
